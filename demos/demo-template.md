# Demo template

## Purpose
Provide structured guidance for conducting interactive demonstrations that allow participants to observe and understand BSV blockchain functionality.

## Learning outcomes
- Observe working BSV blockchain functionality in action.
- Understand the relationship between theoretical concepts and practical implementation.
- Gain confidence through guided interaction with blockchain tools.

## Prerequisites
- List required software, accounts, or setup steps.
- Specify participant experience level needed.
- Note any hardware or network requirements.

## Demo setup
- Environment preparation steps for instructor.
- Required tools and services configuration.
- Backup plans if primary tools fail.

## Step-by-step instructions
1. **Introduction**: explain what participants will see and learn.
2. **Setup verification**: confirm all participants can access required tools.
3. **Demonstration steps**: clear, numbered instructions for each action.
4. **Participant interaction**: points where participants follow along.
5. **Results verification**: how to confirm the demo worked correctly.

## Expected outcomes
- What participants should observe during the demonstration.
- Key insights they should gain from the experience.
- How this connects to broader workshop objectives.

## Common issues and troubleshooting
- Typical problems that occur during demonstrations.
- Quick fixes for common setup or connectivity issues.
- Fallback options when primary demonstration fails.

## Discussion points
- Questions to ask participants about what they observed.
- Connections to theoretical concepts covered earlier.
- Preparation for subsequent workshop activities.

## References
- Links to tools and services used in the demonstration.
- Additional resources for participants who want to explore further.
