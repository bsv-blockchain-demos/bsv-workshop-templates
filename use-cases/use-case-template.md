# Use case template

## Purpose
Provide a structured approach for documenting and analysing potential applications of BSV blockchain technology in specific business or technical contexts.

## Use case overview
- **Name**: concise, descriptive name for the use case.
- **Domain**: business sector or technical area where this applies.
- **Problem statement**: what challenge or opportunity this use case addresses.
- **Proposed solution**: how BSV blockchain functionality provides value.

## Stakeholders and actors
- **Primary users**: who directly interacts with the blockchain functionality.
- **Secondary stakeholders**: others who benefit from or are affected by the solution.
- **System actors**: external systems or services that integrate with the solution.

## Current state analysis
- **Existing processes**: how this is currently handled without blockchain.
- **Pain points**: specific problems with current approaches.
- **Costs and inefficiencies**: quantifiable issues that could be improved.

## Proposed blockchain solution
- **Core functionality**: how BSV blockchain features address the use case.
- **Data requirements**: what information needs to be stored or verified on-chain.
- **Transaction patterns**: types and frequency of blockchain interactions.
- **Integration points**: how this connects with existing systems.

## Value proposition
- **Direct benefits**: immediate advantages from blockchain implementation.
- **Indirect benefits**: secondary effects and broader impact.
- **Cost considerations**: implementation and operational costs.
- **Risk factors**: potential challenges or limitations to consider.

## Technical requirements
- **Blockchain features**: specific BSV capabilities required (payments, data storage, etc.).
- **Performance needs**: transaction throughput, confirmation times, data volumes.
- **Security requirements**: privacy, authentication, and integrity needs.
- **Compliance considerations**: regulatory or standards requirements.

## Implementation approach
- **Development phases**: suggested approach for building and deploying the solution.
- **Pilot strategy**: how to test and validate the use case with real users.
- **Scaling plan**: path from prototype to production deployment.

## Success metrics
- **Key performance indicators**: how to measure success of the blockchain solution.
- **User adoption metrics**: indicators of user acceptance and engagement.
- **Business impact measures**: quantifiable business benefits achieved.

## Related use cases
- Similar applications in other domains or contexts.
- Complementary use cases that could be combined.
- Alternative approaches or competing solutions.

## References
- Industry reports and market analysis relevant to this use case.
- Technical standards and specifications that apply.
- Case studies of similar implementations.
