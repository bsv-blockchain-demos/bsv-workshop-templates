# Introduction to the BSV blockchain

## Purpose
Set a clear context for the workshop by outlining what the BSV blockchain is in neutral, factual terms.

## Learning outcomes
- Recognise core components: blocks, transactions, and the unspent transaction output (UTXO) model.
- Understand at a high level how transactions are formed and validated without committing to specific implementations.
- Know the scope and limits of this workshop’s introductory material.

## Key concepts
- Blocks and transactions: define at a high level without implementation specifics.
- UTXO model: explain the idea of inputs, outputs, and balance derivation in simple terms.
- Fees and confirmations: note that transactions include fees and are confirmed by inclusion in blocks.

## What this section is and is not
- Is: a concise, neutral introduction focused on concepts needed for later sections.
- Is not: a deep technical specification, endorsement, or comparison with other networks.

## Terminology
- Use “BSV blockchain” when referring to the network and “BSV” when referring to the token.
- Prefer plain language and avoid ambiguous or promotional wording.
