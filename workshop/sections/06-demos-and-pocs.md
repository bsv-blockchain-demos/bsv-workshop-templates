# Demos and proofs of concept

## Purpose
Provide structured demonstrations that allow participants to interact with BSV blockchain functionality through practical examples.

## Learning outcomes
- Experience using BSV blockchain features through guided interactions.
- Understand how theoretical concepts translate to working applications.
- Gain confidence through successful completion of practical tasks.

## Demo environment setup
- Prerequisites: list required software, accounts, or access credentials.
- Environment preparation: steps to configure development or testing environments.
- Verification: checks to confirm setup is working correctly.

## Demo structure
Each demonstration should include:
- **Objective**: what the demo proves or teaches.
- **Prerequisites**: what participants need before starting.
- **Step-by-step instructions**: clear, numbered steps to follow.
- **Expected outcomes**: what participants should observe.
- **Verification**: how to confirm the demo worked correctly.

## Demo categories
- **Interactive demonstrations**: participants follow guided steps.
- **Observational demonstrations**: instructor shows functionality.
- **Self-paced exercises**: participants work independently with provided materials.

## Success criteria
- Clear instructions that participants can follow without confusion.
- Verifiable outcomes that participants can check independently.
- Troubleshooting guidance for common issues.
- Time estimates for each demonstration.

## Post-demo activities
- Discussion of what was observed.
- Connection to theoretical concepts covered earlier.
- Questions and clarification.
- Preparation for subsequent workshop sections.

- Note any external services or accounts required.
- Provide fallback options if primary tools are unavailable.
