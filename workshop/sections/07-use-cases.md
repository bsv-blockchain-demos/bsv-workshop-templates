# Use cases

## Purpose
Provide context about practical applications where the primitive demonstrated in this workshop may be relevant.

## Learning outcomes
- Recognise scenarios where BSV blockchain functionality provides specific benefits.
- Understand decision factors for evaluating blockchain-based approaches.
- Connect workshop content to real-world application patterns.

## Use case categories
- **Digital payments**: direct peer-to-peer value transfer without intermediaries.
- **Data integrity**: tamper-evident storage and verification of information.
- **Asset tokenisation**: digital representation and transfer of ownership.
- **Identity verification**: cryptographic proof of identity or credentials.
- **Supply chain tracking**: transparent recording of product provenance.
- **Smart contracts**: automated execution of agreement terms.

## Evaluation criteria
When considering BSV blockchain solutions, consider:
- **Immutability requirements**: does the use case benefit from tamper-evident records?
- **Transparency needs**: would public verifiability add value?
- **Cost considerations**: are transaction fees appropriate for the value being transferred?
- **Performance requirements**: do throughput and latency meet application needs?
- **Regulatory context**: are there compliance requirements to address?

## Common patterns
- **Proof of existence**: timestamping documents or data without storing full content.
- **Micropayments**: enabling small-value transactions not viable with traditional payment systems.
- **Digital certificates**: issuing verifiable credentials or qualifications.
- **Audit trails**: creating transparent logs of business processes.
- **Access control**: using token ownership to gate access to services.

## What this section covers
- High-level scenarios relevant to the workshop primitive.
- Decision-making factors for blockchain adoption.
- Connection between technical features and business value.

## What this section does not cover
- Detailed implementation guidance for production systems.
- Business case analysis or return on investment calculations.
- Regulatory or legal advice for specific jurisdictions.
