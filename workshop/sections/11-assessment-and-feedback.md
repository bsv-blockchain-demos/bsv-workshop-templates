# Assessment and feedback

## Purpose
Provide structured methods to verify that workshop objectives have been met and collect feedback for continuous improvement.

## Learning verification
- **Practical completion**: confirm participants successfully completed core exercises.
- **Understanding checks**: verify comprehension through brief questions or explanations.
- **Application demonstration**: participants show they can apply concepts to new scenarios.

## Assessment methods
Based on workshop objectives, participants should demonstrate:
- **Functional completion**: implemented solutions that work as specified.
- **Conceptual understanding**: ability to explain what their code does and why.
- **Problem-solving ability**: capacity to troubleshoot issues independently.
- **Extension capability**: readiness to build on workshop foundations.

## Knowledge checks
Simple verification questions aligned to learning outcomes:
- Can you explain the key concept covered in this workshop in your own words?
- What are the main steps to implement the demonstrated functionality?
- Where would you look for help if you encountered an unexpected error?
- How might you extend or modify the example for a different use case?

## Completion criteria
Participants successfully complete the workshop when they:
- Finish the required practical exercises with working results.
- Demonstrate understanding through knowledge check responses.
- Show they can locate and use relevant documentation or troubleshooting resources.

## Feedback collection
- **Content relevance**: was the material appropriate for your needs and experience level?
- **Clarity and pacing**: were explanations clear and was the pace suitable?
- **Practical value**: do you feel prepared to apply what you learned?
- **Improvements**: what would make this workshop more effective?

## Feedback methods
- Brief written responses to key questions.
- Group discussion of main takeaways and suggestions.
- Individual reflection on learning goals and next steps.

## Follow-up resources
- Relevant documentation for continued learning.
- Community forums or support channels.
- Suggested next topics or advanced workshops.
- Contact information for workshop-related questions.

## What this section covers
- Objective methods to verify learning outcomes.
- Systematic collection of participant feedback.
- Clear completion criteria and next steps.

## What this section does not cover
- Formal certification or accreditation processes.
- Detailed grading rubrics for academic assessment.
- Long-term tracking of participant progress.
